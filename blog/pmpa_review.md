# **TCM Security - Practical Mobile Pentest Associate: Course and Exam Review**

_By magnafoco 🔥 - December 2025_ 

---

Hey folks! I recently smashed through the **Practical Mobile Pentest Associate** (PMPA) cert from TCM Security, and I'd like to share my experience with the course and exam. 
If you're thinking about diving into mobile pentesting or just want to level up your offensive security game, keep reading.

## Course Overview

The PMPA course is well-structured and provides comprehensive coverage of mobile penetration testing for both iOS and Android platforms. The instructional videos are delivered by Aaron Wilson, a qualified instructor who presents the material clearly and effectively. 
The course is designed to equip students with practical knowledge and hands-on skills necessary to conduct thorough mobile application penetration tests.

The course covers a wide range of essential topics:
- Lab setup for iOS and Android testing
- Static analysis and hunting for hardcoded secrets
- Automated analysis with MobSF
- Dynamic analysis and traffic interception
- Bypassing SSL pinning (Frida & Objection)
- Runtime manipulation and hooking techniques
- OWASP Mobile Top 10

## The Exam Experience

The PMPA exam simulates a real-world Android mobile application penetration test. Your mission is to identify as many vulnerabilities as possible within the target application, with the ultimate goal of escalating privileges to gain administrative access on the backend API server.

The course material provides solid preparation for the exam, covering all the necessary techniques and methodologies.
However, I would recommend having some foundational knowledge of API penetration testing to maximize your success, particularly for achieving the bonus objectives. Understanding common API vulnerabilities and testing approaches will give you an edge.

### My Experience

Drawing from my existing penetration testing experience, I managed to pwn the exam in about 3 hours, but honestly time flew by because I was enjoying the challenge. This is the kind of certification that focuses on hands-on abilities rather than just theoretical knowledge.

## Final Thoughts

The PMPA certification is a solid choice for penetration testers looking to add mobile application testing to their arsenal. 
The course is practical, the instructions are clear, and the exam provides a genuine test of your capabilities. Whether you're new to mobile penetration testing or looking to formalize your existing skills, this certification offers real value.

If you're considering the PMPA, my advice is to thoroughly practice the techniques covered in the course and brush up on API testing fundamentals. With proper preparation, it's an achievable and rewarding certification that demonstrates practical mobile security skills.

Congrats, now you'll never trust a mobile app again. Welcome to the club! 🔥📱
